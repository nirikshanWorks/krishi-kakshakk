The Real Problems Farmers Face

Every day, hardworking farmers lose money to an outdated system

Working the Hardest, Earning the Least

Farmers struggle with unpredictable weather, pest attacks, and market price fluctuations. Heavy dependence on middlemen significantly reduces their profits despite their backbreaking work.

  • Price Exploitation from middlemen
  • Zero Digital Reach
  • Limited access to modern technology

Incredible Products, Invisible Sellers

Self-help groups produce high-quality organic products but lack a permanent platform to sell them. Without digital presence and formal certifications, their reach remains limited.

  • No permanent marketplace access
  • Unverified Quality standards
  • Limited buyer trust

Willing to Pay, Unable to Trust

Buyers struggle to find verified, reliable sources of fresh produce. The broken supply chain makes it impossible to trace products to their origin or verify authenticity.

  • Supply chain transparency issues
  • Product authenticity verification
  • Logistics complexity

Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to get started

Does it really cost nothing?

Yes. Signing up is free. Listing your crops is free. We don't charge commission on your sales either. You keep 100% of what you sell. We make money when buyers use premium features, not by taking cuts from farmers.

How do I actually get paid?

Once a buyer confirms they want your crops and they're delivered, payment goes straight to your bank account within 24-48 hours. No waiting weeks. No middleman delays. Direct transfer to whatever bank account you register with.

What if I don't have a smartphone?

You can use our web version on any computer with internet. We also have phone support — call our helpline and agents can help you. Plus, we're working on SMS and voice call features so you can use the platform however works best for you.

How does transport work?

Once a buyer confirms an order, you request transport through our app. Our network of verified transporters quotes prices. You pick one, they handle the delivery, and you pay them. It's transparent, tracked in real-time, and direct between you and the transporter.

What languages do you support?

We support 15+ Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, and more. You can switch your preferred language anytime in settings. The goal is for every farmer to feel comfortable using the platform in their own language.

How accurate is the AI crop disease detection?

Our AI is trained on thousands of crop images and catches diseases with >90% accuracy. Just take a clear photo of the affected leaf or area, upload it, and get results in seconds. It suggests treatments and tells you where to get medicine. It's not 100% perfect but catches most problems early enough to save your crop.
